The Classic Tsiribihina Descent

Three days on the river that cuts through the wild west of Madagascar: gorges and red cliffs, a waterfall to swim under, Sakalava villages, lemurs in the riverside trees and two nights camping on sandbanks.

Itinerary

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Miandrivazo — onto the river

    Meet your crew in Miandrivazo and board your pirogue or chaland. Lunch on the water as the river enters its first gorges. First camp on a quiet sandbank, dinner cooked fresh by the camp crew.

  2. Day 2

    The heart of the Tsiribihina

    A full day on the river: swim at the Anosin'Ampela waterfall and natural pool, look for lemurs, herons and kingfishers along the banks, and visit a Sakalava village. In the evening, a big campfire on a wild sandbank and a night under the stars.

  3. Day 3

    Down to Belo-sur-Tsiribihina

    A last calm stretch of river at sunrise, then arrival in Belo-sur-Tsiribihina. Your 4x4 is waiting to take you to Morondava, Kirindy Forest or the Tsingy.

The river days are the same on every trip; the exact program is adapted to your dates, the water level and your pace.
