About us

A local team, one river

We focus on one thing: taking you down the Tsiribihina safely, comfortably and with the people who know it best.

Why one river?

Many agencies sell the Tsiribihina as one item in a long list. We decided to specialise in it. We are based in Morondava, a few hours from the river, and we organise everything ourselves: the boat and its crew, the camps, the meals and the 4x4 transfers before and after the river.

Who takes you down the river

Our pirogues are paddled by Malagasy boatmen from the river villages. For chaland trips, we work with trusted operators on the Tsiribihina, including Dafimavo, La Sirène and Lakana Be. On every trip you have a local guide, a camp crew who cook fresh meals, security staff and life jackets.

How we work

  • One clear quote, with exactly what is included
  • Private trips — only your group in the boat and at camp
  • A real person answers you on WhatsApp, Monday to Saturday, 8:00–22:00
  • We stay in touch from your first message until you are back from the river

Ready for the river?

Tell us your dates, how many you are and which boat you prefer. We reply on WhatsApp with a clear program and a free quote, usually within a few hours.
