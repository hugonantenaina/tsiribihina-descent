Frequently asked questions

How long is the Tsiribihina River descent?

The classic descent from Miandrivazo to Belo-sur-Tsiribihina takes 3 days and 2 nights on the river. You can add Kirindy Forest, the Avenue of the Baobabs or the Tsingy de Bemaraha after the river.

What is the difference between a pirogue and a chaland?

A pirogue is a narrow traditional wooden canoe paddled by boatmen: silent and close to the water. A chaland is a large motorised boat with a roof: more space, shade from sun and rain, and room for luggage — ideal for families and groups.

When can you do the Tsiribihina descent?

From about April to November, during the dry season. June to October has the most reliable weather. From December to March, the rains make the river and the roads unpredictable.

Where do we sleep on the river?

In tents on sandbanks along the river. The camp crew sets up the camp, cooks the meals and provides an enclosed toilet. The second night includes a big campfire.

Is the river trip safe for children?

Yes, with the right boat. Families usually choose the chaland: it is stable and spacious, and life jackets are provided for everyone.

Do I need to be fit?

No. On the river you sit in the boat while the crew paddles or drives. Short walks to the waterfall and villages are easy. Only the Grand Tsingy, on some longer trips, needs a good level of fitness.

How do I get to Miandrivazo?

Miandrivazo is a day's drive from Antananarivo (via Antsirabe) or from Morondava. We arrange the private 4x4 transfer as part of your trip.

Can we continue to the Tsingy after the river?

Yes. From Belo-sur-Tsiribihina, the road north leads to Bekopaka and the Tsingy de Bemaraha, which is usually open from about mid-May to mid-November.

How much does the trip cost?

The price depends on the boat (pirogue or chaland), the number of travellers and what you add after the river. Send us your dates and group size on WhatsApp for a free, detailed quote.

What should I bring?

Light clothes with long sleeves, a warm layer for the evenings, a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, a head torch, swimwear, sandals and a dry bag for your camera and phone.
