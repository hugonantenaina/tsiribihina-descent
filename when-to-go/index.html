The best time for the Tsiribihina

River trips run in the dry season, from about April to November. June to October gives the most reliable weather; April and May are greener and quieter. From December to March, the rains make the river and the dirt roads of the west unpredictable.

Getting to Miandrivazo

The descent starts in Miandrivazo, a day's drive from Antananarivo (through Antsirabe) or from Morondava. We arrange a private 4x4 with driver as part of your trip, and pick you up again in Belo-sur-Tsiribihina at the end of the river.

What to bring

  • Light clothes with long sleeves and trousers (sun and mosquitoes)
  • A warm layer for the evenings and early mornings
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Insect repellent
  • Head torch with spare batteries
  • Swimwear, quick-dry towel and sandals
  • A dry bag for your phone, camera and documents
  • Personal medicine and a small first-aid kit
  • Cash in ariary for tips and village purchases

We provide tents, mattresses, meals, drinking water and life jackets.
